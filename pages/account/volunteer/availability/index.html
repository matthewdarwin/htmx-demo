This form allows you to indicate when you are available to volunteer. Once you have completed this form, one of the volunteer coordinators may schedule you in to a particular movie. If you are scheduled, you will receive an e-mail on Wednesday at 8:30pm with the list of movies for which you are scheduled.

Definitions:

  • Available = I am free and I can work at this show.
  • Unavailable = Sorry, I am not available to work at this show.
  • In an Emergency = I am free to work at this show, but please keep me as a backup.
  • Unknown = I have not decided if I am free or not.

Note: you can set your default availability and Unknown movies will be changed to the default you specify.
