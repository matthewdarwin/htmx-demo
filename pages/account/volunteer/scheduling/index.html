Schedule Availability lists upcoming shows where we'd like to know if you're available to volunteer. Once you've indicated your availability, one of the volunteer coordinators may schedule you in for a particular movie. If you're scheduled, you'll receive an e-mail on Wednesday at 8:30pm with the list of movies for which you're scheduled.

Definitions:

  • Available = I am free and I can work at this show.
  • Unavailable = Sorry, I am not available to work at this show.
  • In an Emergency = I am free to work at this show, but please keep me as a backup.
  • Unknown = I have not decided if I am free or not.

Schedule shows which positions you're actually assigned to for upcoming shows.

Default Availability is your standing preference for whether you're generally available on Fridays and Saturdays — Unknown dates on Schedule Availability will automatically be changed to whatever you've set there.
