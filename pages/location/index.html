Please note: The Ottawa Family Cinema has moved to Rideau Community Hub at 815 St-Laurent Boulevard. The old location at 710 Broadview Avenue is closed.

Ottawa Family Cinema is located at Rideau Community Hub, 815 St-Laurent Boulevard, Ottawa, Ontario, K1K 3A7. Use the main entrance facing St-Laurent Boulevard.

OC Transpo bus route 7 will drop you off on St-Laurent Boulevard near the building.

Directions for people coming via the Queensway:

  1. exit at St-Laurent Boulevard
  2. go North on St-Laurent
  3. turn right into Rideau Hub

There is free parking in the lot behind the auditorium and on nearby streets.
